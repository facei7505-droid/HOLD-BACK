Retention money nobody can sit on

A client keeps 5–10% of every payment as a warranty for one or two years. Holdback locks that money in a Solana vault with no key, freezes only what a defect costs, and lets anyone release the rest the day the warranty ends.

Four rules the program enforces

01 · SPLIT

Pay and lock in one transaction

Each invoice splits 95% to the subcontractor and 5% to the vault. Both transfers succeed or neither does.

02 · VAULT

No key, no owner

The vault is owned by the contract's program address. Only the program can move the money, and only by the rules.

03 · DEFECT

Freeze only the repair

The client freezes the cost of one defect with an evidence hash. An arbiter pays the repair or unfreezes it.

04 · RELEASE

Anyone can pay it out

After the warranty date, with no open defect, any wallet or a Blink link sends the balance to its owner.

BONUS · SELL

Cash today

The subcontractor can sell the locked claim. The funder pays and becomes the beneficiary in the same transaction.
